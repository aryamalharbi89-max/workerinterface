Need Help?
We are here to help you with locker selection, package scanning, and delivery confirmation.
Quick Answers
I cannot select a locker
Make sure the locker is available and that its size matches your parcel size.
QR scan is not working
Reposition the package QR inside the frame and try again. Make sure the selected locker is correct.
Locker did not respond
Go back to locker selection and retry, or select another available locker.
Tip
Before continuing, confirm the parcel size matches the selected locker and make sure the locker is marked as available.
