Courier Privacy Policy
This page explains how UniBox handles courier-related operational and personal data.
Policy Details

Courier Data Collection

UniBox collects limited courier information such as account identity, delivery activity, locker access logs, and parcel handoff events to support delivery operations.

Purpose of Use

Courier data is used to verify deliveries, monitor locker usage, improve route efficiency, and maintain secure access to UniBox compartments.

Access & Security

Courier information is stored securely and can only be viewed by authorized UniBox administrators and system operators responsible for logistics and support.

Service Protection

Activity logs may be reviewed to investigate delivery issues, locker misuse, technical failures, or unusual access attempts related to courier operations.
