Top FAQs
How do I open my locker?
Go to the active order, choose the correct locker, scan the QR code, and follow the on-screen instructions.
I cannot scan the QR code.
Make sure the QR is centered in the scan frame and the selected locker matches your order details.
The locker did not open.
Retry from the locker selection page or choose another available locker. If the problem continues, contact support.
How do I know the correct locker size?
The system automatically shows the allowed locker sizes based on the parcel size for your order.
